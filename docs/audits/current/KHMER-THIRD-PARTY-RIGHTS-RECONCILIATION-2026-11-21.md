# Khmer third-party source-rights reconciliation — 2026-11-21

**Status:** source-level triage advanced; item-level and derivative rights remain PARTIAL/BLOCKED. This is a research audit, not legal advice or blanket clearance.

## Decision model

Assess each component independently: source orthography/text; dictionary entries; IPA transcription; audio; speaker/consent data; model weights; software; derived labels; compiled database. A paper's publication license does not automatically license its dataset. A software license does not automatically license an associated pronunciation dictionary, audio corpus or trained model. Mixed corpora require provenance and rights decisions at the component/row level.

Use statuses:
- CLEARED_FOR_PILOT: evidence supports the specified internal research/annotation use and retention.
- CLEARED_FOR_REDISTRIBUTION: explicit rights support the intended public redistribution and derivative terms.
- RESTRICTED: permitted only within stated conditions (e.g., non-commercial/share-alike).
- UNKNOWN: insufficient or absent permission evidence; exclude from intake/export.
- CONFLICT: authoritative records disagree; quarantine until maintainer clarification.
- EXCLUDED: outside project scope or not a pronunciation source.

## Mixed-corpus rule

A single top-level license string is not sufficient when a dataset combines multiple origins. Keep separate source rows and exact locators; record translation/adaptation status; retain original license text and attribution; check whether annotations, derived labels, model weights and database compilation have distinct rights. If any component is UNKNOWN or CONFLICT, exclude that component from public gold and any model training/export that would reproduce or depend on it until resolved. For personal-data sources, license alone is not a lawful basis or consent.

## Required evidence per pilot item

source_id; original publisher/maintainer; exact source title and edition/version/date; stable URL and exact page/entry/audio locator; artifact SHA-256 where lawful; underlying source work and translation lineage; dataset-specific license identifier/version and evidence URL; rights holder/permission contact; permitted internal research, annotation, retention, adaptation, redistribution and commercial use; attribution text; share-alike/NC obligations; privacy/consent restrictions; reviewer/date; final status; reason for exclusion where not cleared.

## Explicit exclusions and stop rules

- Do not import rows with UNKNOWN or CONFLICT into the public gold corpus.
- Do not treat MekongPhon generated sentence pairs as independently transcribed sentence-level gold.
- Do not treat Khmer ALT/khPOS tokenization as pronunciation evidence or use NC-SA data in an unrestricted commercial product.
- Do not use student/voter-list data without privacy/provenance clearance.
- Do not infer dictionary/model rights from software MIT license.
- Do not infer audio/weight rights from the paper's publication license.
- Do not claim third-party rights are complete until all selected pilot items and intended redistributed components have item-level decisions.

## Current acceptance

Source-level triage is improved, but no new item has been cleared for the 30-item pilot by this audit. Third-party rights remain PARTIAL; gold remains 0; accuracy remains unmeasured.
